Calculus II AI Study Companion

Personal project · Spring 2025

Overview

I built an AI-powered study companion to help students practice and understand Calculus II.

What it does

  • Generates practice problems on Calculus II topics
  • Gives step-by-step explanations and personalized feedback
  • Organizes topics and examples so students can find their weak areas

Why I built it

As a Supplemental Instruction Leader, I saw how many students struggled because they didn’t know which topics they needed to practice. This tool was my way of giving students more targeted practice outside of tutoring sessions.
