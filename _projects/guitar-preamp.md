**Status:** In progress, prototyping on a breadboard

I'm building a small, portable guitar preamp so I can practice without playing through a loud amp.

## Design

The build follows the [Portable Guitar Preamp](https://www.instructables.com/Portable-Guitar-Preamp){:target="_blank"} guide on Instructables and adds the [Tonemender](https://runoffgroove.com/tonemender.html){:target="_blank"} tonestack from runoffgroove.

## Progress

I'm prototyping the circuit on a breadboard first, so I can test it and make changes before committing to a final build.
